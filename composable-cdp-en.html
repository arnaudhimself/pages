Straight answers

Your stack. Your rules.

Do we need to replace our CRM or warehouse?

No. Your existing systems remain the source of truth for the data they own. Commanders Act uses synchronized context to enrich and activate live events.

How do zero-copy and the operational cache work together?

Your CRM or warehouse keeps the reference history. The attributes needed for activation are copied and refreshed in an operational cache at Commanders Act. The real time engine uses this context and live events to evaluate segments.

Where are our segments evaluated?

In the Commanders Act real time engine, using live events and the latest synchronized customer context. Historical calculations prepared in your systems can feed these segments.

How fresh is the customer context?

It depends on the frequency and success of synchronization. Live events are processed as they arrive. CRM or warehouse context reflects the latest available synchronization.
